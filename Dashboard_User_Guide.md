## 1. Getting Started

Welcome to the **EduVision_DV Higher Education Performance Dashboard Suite**. This interactive Tableau workbook provides comprehensive insights into global university performance, research metrics, student demographics, and country-level benchmarks.

### File Requirements
- **Tableau Version**: Tableau Desktop / Tableau Reader / Tableau Public 2023.1 or newer.
- **Workbook File**: `EduVision_DV.twbx` (Packaged workbook containing data extracts).

---

## 2. Navigating the 4 Dashboards

The workbook is organized into 4 interconnected dashboards accessible via the top navigation bar or workbook tabs:

### 1. University Overview Dashboard (`Dashboard 1`)
- **Key Features**: Global university rankings, top 10 institutions scorecard, regional distribution donut chart, academic reputation score breakdown.
- **How to Use**:
  - Filter by `Year`, `Region`, or `Country` using top dropdown controls.
  - Review top-level KPIs in the header ribbon (Top Ranking, Total Universities, Avg Score, Int'l Students %, Faculty Ratio).

### 2. Research Analytics Dashboard (`Dashboard 2`)
- **Key Features**: Citations per faculty, research environment quality, publication volume, quadrant scatter plot (Research Impact vs. Citations).
- **How to Use**:
  - Hover over data points in the scatter chart to inspect individual university citations and impact scores.
  - Filter by region to isolate research hubs in Europe, North America, or Asia.

### 3. Student Analytics Dashboard (`Dashboard 3`)
- **Key Features**: International student percentage map, faculty-to-student ratios, diversity trends, enrollment breakdowns.
- **How to Use**:
  - Click on any country on the world map to highlight that country's student statistics.
  - Compare faculty-to-student accessibility across institutions.

### 4. Country Comparison Dashboard (`Dashboard 4`)
- **Key Features**: National education performance benchmarks, average QS score by country, citations per faculty by country, industry income metrics.
- **How to Use**:
  - Compare country-level performance matrices.
  - Analyze regional trends over evaluation periods.

---

## 3. Global Controls & Interactivity

- **Global Filter Panel**: Located at the top of each dashboard. Changes made here apply across all relevant views.
- **Reset Filters Button**: Click `Reset Filters` to restore default view (`All` years, regions, and countries).
- **Tooltips**: Hover over any chart element for detailed institution profiles.
- **Exporting Views**: To export charts for presentations, select `File` -> `Export As Image` or `Export PDF` in Tableau.
