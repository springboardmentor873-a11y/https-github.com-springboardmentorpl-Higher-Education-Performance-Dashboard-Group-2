DATA COLLECTION PROCESS

The following steps were completed:

1. Loaded the QS World University Rankings 2026 CSV dataset.

2. Loaded the THE World University Rankings 2026 Excel dataset.

3. Inspected the number of records and columns in both datasets.

4. Identified the university name, country, ranking and overall score
   fields from both ranking systems.

5. Standardized the column names used by both datasets.

6. Selected the required university performance indicators.

7. Added the ranking year as 2026.

8. Added a ranking_source field to identify the source of each record.

9. Standardized both datasets into the same common structure.

10. Combined the QS and THE records into one raw dataset.

11. Removed exact duplicate records.

12. Checked missing values in all required fields.

13. Calculated dataset completeness.

14. Saved the final raw integrated dataset as
    university_raw_data.csv.

FIELD DESCRIPTIONS

university_name:

The name of the university.


country:

The country or territory associated with the university.


year:

The ranking year.

The current datasets represent the 2026 rankings.


ranking_source:

Identifies the ranking system from which the record was collected.

Possible values:

QS
THE


rank:

The ranking position of the university in the corresponding ranking
system.


score:

The overall performance score provided by the corresponding ranking
system.


DATA INTEGRATION APPROACH

QS and THE use different column names and have different university
coverage.

Instead of forcing the two datasets into a side-by-side structure,
both datasets were converted into a common long-format structure.

Example:

University A | Country X | 2026 | QS  | Rank | Score

University A | Country X | 2026 | THE | Rank | Score

This approach preserves the original ranking source and prevents
artificial missing values.
