# EduVision — KPI Definitions

## 1. Total Universities

**Definition:**  
Total number of universities represented in the dataset.

**Purpose:**  
Shows the overall number of universities included in the analysis.

---

## 2. Total Countries

**Definition:**  
Total number of countries represented in the dataset.

**Purpose:**  
Shows the geographical coverage of the dataset.

---

## 3. Average Overall Score

**Definition:**  
Average value of the Overall Score across the selected universities or countries.

**Purpose:**  
Provides a general measure of university performance.

---

## 4. Average Global Ranking

**Definition:**  
Average Global Ranking value across the selected records.

**Purpose:**  
Provides an overall view of global ranking performance.

---

## 5. Average Research Impact

**Definition:**  
Average Research Impact score across the selected universities.

**Purpose:**  
Measures the research influence and impact represented in the dataset.

---

## 6. Research Productivity

**Definition:**  
Measure representing research productivity for universities.

**Purpose:**  
Used to compare research output and productivity between institutions.

---

## 7. International Students

**Definition:**  
Percentage or metric representing international student participation.

**Purpose:**  
Used to analyze the international presence of universities.

---

## 8. Faculty-to-Student Ratio

**Definition:**  
Metric representing the relationship between faculty and students.

**Purpose:**  
Used to compare faculty availability relative to students.

---

## 9. Academic Reputation

**Definition:**  
Score representing the academic reputation of universities.

**Purpose:**  
Used to compare institutions based on academic reputation.

---

## 10. Country Global Ranking

**Definition:**  
Aggregated ranking metric used for country-level comparison.

**Purpose:**  
Used to compare educational performance between countries.

---

## 11. KPI Usage

The KPIs are presented through Tableau KPI cards, charts, maps, and comparison visualizations.

Users can interact with the dashboards using filters and selections to analyze different countries and educational performance metrics.
