# EduVision KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Dashboard Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Total Universities | Number of universities included in the dataset | Overview / Country |
| Total Countries | Number of countries represented | Home / Country |
| Overall Score | Indicator of overall university performance | All dashboards |
| Global Ranking | Indicator used for global ranking comparison | Overview / Research / Student |
| Research Impact | Indicator of research influence/performance | Research |
| Research Productivity | Indicator of research productivity | Research |
| Academic Reputation | Indicator of academic reputation | University Overview |
| Faculty-to-Student Ratio | Indicator representing faculty/student relationship | Student |
| International Student Percentage | Percentage of international students | Student |
| Country Global Ranking | Country-level ranking indicator | Country Comparison |

## KPI Purpose

The KPIs provide quick summary information and allow users to compare universities and countries using standardized indicators.

## KPI Validation

All KPI values used in the dashboards were reviewed during the testing and validation stage.
