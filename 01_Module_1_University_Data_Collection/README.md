# Module 1 – University Data Collection

## EduVision_DV | Higher Education Performance Dashboard

### 1. Module Overview

Module 1 focuses on the **collection and initial integration of university ranking data** required for the EduVision_DV Higher Education Performance Dashboard.

The objective of this module is to gather publicly available university ranking datasets, collect relevant institutional performance indicators, and prepare the raw data for subsequent cleaning, transformation, and KPI engineering.

The primary ranking sources used in this project are:

- QS World University Rankings
- Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings

The collected datasets form the foundation for the remaining EduVision_DV modules.

---

## 2. Module Objectives

The main objectives of Module 1 are:

- Collect university ranking datasets from the identified sources.
- Download and organize QS World University Rankings data.
- Download and organize Times Higher Education World University Rankings data.
- Collect relevant university performance indicators.
- Examine the structure and fields of the collected datasets.
- Integrate the ranking datasets into a common raw-data structure.
- Preserve the collected information for further processing in Module 2.

---

## 3. Data Sources

### QS World University Rankings

The QS World University Rankings dataset provides university-level information used to analyze institutional ranking and academic performance.

Relevant information includes university identification, country information, ranking information, and available performance indicators.

### Times Higher Education World University Rankings

The Times Higher Education World University Rankings dataset provides additional university performance and ranking information.

The dataset contains institutional indicators that support the analysis of research, academic, student, and international performance.

> **Note:** The datasets are used as publicly available sources for educational analytics and dashboard development.
